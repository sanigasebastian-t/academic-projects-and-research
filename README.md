# Academic Projects & Research

This repository showcases selected academic projects and practical work completed as part of my **Master of Applied Information Technology at Waikato Institute of Technology (Wintec), New Zealand**.

My academic interests include Business Analysis, Business Intelligence, Data Analytics, Software Testing and IT Project Management.

## Academic Projects

### 1. E-commerce Customer Behaviour and Sales Forecasting

**Area:** Data Analytics & Machine Learning

- Analysed an e-commerce dataset to explore customer behaviour and sales patterns.
- Used Python, Pandas and Random Forest modelling.
- Compared forecasting performance using MAE and RMSE.
- Explored how behavioural features can contribute to sales forecasting.

**Technologies:** Python, Pandas, Random Forest

### 2. POS End-of-Day Cash Reconciliation Testing

**Area:** Software Testing & Automation

- Developed a demonstration of a point-of-sale end-of-day cash reconciliation process.
- Designed and executed functional test cases.
- Explored automated testing using Selenium WebDriver and pytest.
- Tested balanced, shortage and overage scenarios.

**Technologies:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, Selenium WebDriver, pytest

### 3. Google Lens – Visual Product Discovery

**Area:** Business Analysis

- Explored visual product discovery for a retail business scenario.
- Developed a business case and evaluated potential solution options.
- Applied business analysis techniques, including SWOT and PESTLE.
- Considered feasibility, stakeholder needs and user experience.

**Focus areas:** Business Analysis, Requirements Analysis, Feasibility Assessment

### 4. Project Management for IT

**Area:** IT Project Management

- Studied project planning and management through an IT project case study.
- Explored project scope, stakeholder management, risks and delivery considerations.
- Applied project management concepts to evaluate project challenges and outcomes.

**Focus areas:** IT Project Management, Risk Management, Stakeholder Management
